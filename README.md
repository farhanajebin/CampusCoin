💸 CampusCoin — Level Up Your Pocket Money!

CampusCoin is a gamified expense-tracking application designed to help college students manage their pocket money, track daily expenses, and build smarter financial habits.

Managing money as a student can be challenging. CampusCoin makes it easier and more engaging by combining expense tracking, budgeting, and game-inspired features in one place.

✨ Features
💰 Budget Management — Set your monthly budget and monitor your spending.
📊 Expense Tracking — Record and manage your daily expenses.
🎯 Savings Goals — Set financial goals and track your progress.
🏆 Gamified Experience — Make managing money more engaging with a game-inspired interface.
🎨 Interactive Dashboard — View your financial information in a clean, student-friendly layout.
🌈 Multiple Themes — Explore different visual themes for a personalized experience.
📈 Spending Insights — Understand your spending habits and make better financial decisions.
🎯 Who Is CampusCoin For?

CampusCoin is designed especially for:

College students managing pocket money.
Students learning to budget for the first time.
Young adults who want to develop better saving habits.
Anyone looking for a simple and engaging way to track expenses.
